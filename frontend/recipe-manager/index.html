Why it works

A calmer way to collect and improve recipes

The app is built around the messy ways recipes show up in real life: shared links, screenshots, half-finished ideas, and dishes you want to adapt for your own kitchen.

Capture recipes from anywhere

Paste a URL, upload a photo, or drop in text and let the app turn it into structured ingredients, steps, timings, and tags.

Keep ideas before they become recipes

Save lightweight recipe ideas with notes and tags so inspiration does not disappear into bookmarks or chat history.

Refine dishes with plain-English edits

Ask for changes like “double the tomatoes” or “this serves 2, not 3” and preview the updated recipe before you save it.

Capture

Bring recipes in however you found them

Most recipe apps assume you start with a perfect import flow. Recipe Manager is more forgiving: save a polished URL, a phone photo, pasted text, or just a rough idea you want to cook later.

  • Import from recipe links with AI extraction
  • Upload photos of cookbooks or notes
  • Paste text directly when the source is messy
  • Store recipe ideas before you know every ingredient

Organise

Build a searchable recipe collection instead of a pile of tabs

Once recipes are saved, they live in a simple collection you can browse by title, search by tag, and return to when you actually need dinner ideas.

  • Browse everything in one clean overview
  • Search by title or cuisine tags
  • Keep source links attached to imported recipes
  • Store recipes in a lightweight SQLite-backed app you control

Refine

Adjust recipes without rewriting them from scratch

The editing flow is built for realistic tweaks: change servings, update ingredients, and reshape a recipe with a plain-English prompt before committing the result.

  • Preview recipe edits before saving
  • Make ingredient and serving changes in natural language
  • Add per-serving calories when they matter
  • Open a calorie breakdown for more detail
